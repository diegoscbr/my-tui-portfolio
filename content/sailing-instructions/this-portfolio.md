An SSH-accessible terminal portfolio at diego.boats — a split-panel TUI with
a sailing regatta theme. Visitors `ssh diego.boats` and get ASCII art on the
left, bio/content sections on the right, tab navigation along the bottom.

Built with Python 3.12, Textual, and asyncssh.
